DeepVoice v2 model inventory

DF-Arena 1B
- Purpose: voice-fake detection and raw/file fake evidence.
- Local directory: df_arena_1b/
- Input: 16 kHz mono, 64,600-sample segments.

HTDemucs
- Purpose: vocal/music source separation.
- Local directory: htdemucs/
- The native 44.1 kHz music stem is preserved for ArtifactNet.

PANNs Cnn14
- Purpose: voice/music component-presence probabilities.
- Local directory: panns/

ArtifactNet v9.4
- Purpose: AI-generated music detection.
- Source: https://huggingface.co/intrect/artifactnet
- Pinned revision: 7c9b753a9d006b48e4bfaf85bf0157e135f4aad4
- Local directory: artifactnet/
- Runtime: ONNX Runtime GPU, with CUDA preferred and CPU fallback.
- Input: 44.1 kHz mono, four-second chunks; track score is the median chunk probability.
- v2 runs ArtifactNet independently on raw audio and the HTDemucs music stem.
